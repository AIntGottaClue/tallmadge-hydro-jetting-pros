Hydro Jetting Along East Avenue

The Record-Courier explains that eight roads meet the Circle, named North, South, East, West, Northeast, Northwest, Southwest and Southeast. East Avenue is the one that runs eastward, so it carried development outward from the center as the city grew.

The city's history page says commercial development followed 1950s suburban patterns, with small convenience centers near new neighborhoods and strip-type centers along major roads. A road like East Avenue can therefore hold houses and small businesses side by side. The Summit County Historical Society describes the Circle at the center of town as the point the roads were built to reach.

A mixed road means mixed plumbing. Jetting suits sound lines with buildup, and an inspection decides.
